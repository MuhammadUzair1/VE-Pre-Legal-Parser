# AI Addendum

## COMMON PAPER AI ADDENDUM (Version 1.0)

### 1. AI Services

#### 1.1 Using AI Services
The AI Services are integrated into the Product and follow the Agreement, supplemented by this AI Addendum. Customers provide Input to access AI Services, which generate Output in response. The Provider may "copy, display, modify, distribute, and use Input to the extent necessary to provide the AI Services" with customer authorization.

#### 1.2 Restrictions
Customers must not:
1. Deploy AI Services for regulated industry decisions without adequate human oversight and legal compliance
2. Use AI Services to violate intellectual property rights
3. Misrepresent AI-generated content as human-created

#### 1.3 Model Training
The Provider cannot use customer Inputs or Outputs for model training unless the Cover Page specifically permits it. When authorized, the Provider may process Training Data according to specified Training Purposes and Training Restrictions.

#### 1.4 Non-Training Improvement
Subject to Improvement Restrictions, the Provider may utilize Input, Output, and Training Data to "provide, maintain, develop, and improve the AI System," excluding Training except where authorized.

### 2. Intellectual Property and Privacy

#### 2.1 Ownership
Customers retain all rights to Input they submit. Customers own all Output generated. The Provider assigns all rights in Output to the customer "to the extent permitted by Applicable Laws."

#### 2.2 Personal Data
This AI Addendum does not diminish the Provider's obligations under data protection laws regarding Personal Data contained in Input.

#### 2.3 Rights to Input
Customers represent they possess all necessary rights to submit Input, including on behalf of Users and other contributors.

### 3. Disclaimers

#### 3.1 Nature of AI
AI-generated information may contain errors. "The AI Services are not human and are not a substitute for human oversight." Output may lack intellectual property protection.

#### 3.2 Similarity of Output
Output may resemble content created for other users. The Provider offers no guarantee that Output avoids incorporating others' materials or infringing their intellectual property rights.

### 4. Key Definitions

- **AI Addendum Standard Terms:** Common Paper AI Addendum Standard Terms Version 1.0
- **AI Services:** Artificial intelligence or machine learning components within the Product
- **AI System:** The AI application and services layers, excluding underlying Models
- **Input:** Data or materials submitted by customers or users to AI Services
- **Model:** Large language, machine learning, or artificial intelligence model
- **Output:** Data or materials the AI Services create in response to Input
- **Train/Training:** Using data to create or improve a Model
- **Improvement Restrictions:** Limitations on using data to improve the AI System
- **Training Purposes:** Specified purposes for which training data may be used
- **Training Restrictions:** Limitations on how training data may be used

### 5. Integration with Main Agreement

This AI Addendum supplements the main Product Agreement. Where conflicts arise:
1. AI Addendum provisions take precedence over conflicting Product Agreement terms
2. All other Product Agreement terms continue to apply
3. The AI Addendum specifically addresses AI/ML components
4. Non-AI provisions are governed by main Agreement

### 6. Important Considerations for Users

#### Risks and Limitations
- AI output may be inaccurate, incomplete, or biased
- Output may require human review and verification
- Provider does not guarantee accuracy or reliability
- Output may unintentionally infringe third-party intellectual property rights

#### Data Privacy
- Users should not submit Personal Data to AI Services unless covered by DPA
- Personal Data in Input is subject to all applicable data protection laws
- Customers remain responsible for compliance with data protection regulations
- Input data handling depends on authorization in Cover Page

#### Intellectual Property
- Customer retains all rights to Input
- Customer owns all Output
- Customer responsible for ensuring Input doesn't violate third-party rights
- Output may contain content similar to other outputs
- No guarantees regarding Output originality or uniqueness

#### Appropriate Use
- Not suitable for safety-critical applications without human oversight
- Cannot be used for deceptive purposes
- Cannot be used to infringe intellectual property
- Customer responsible for appropriate use determination
- Human review and oversight strongly recommended
