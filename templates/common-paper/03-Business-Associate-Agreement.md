# Business Associate Agreement

## COMMON PAPER BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT (Version 1.0)

### 1. Business Associate Obligations

#### 1.1 Permitted Uses and Disclosures
Provider may only use or disclose PHI as reasonably necessary to provide the Services or as otherwise required by applicable law.

#### 1.2 Privacy and Information Security Program
Provider must maintain a program ensuring workforce compliance, including:
- Training on PHI protection standards
- Policies meeting current HIPAA requirements
- Appointing Privacy and Security Officials per HIPAA requirements

#### 1.3 Safeguards
"Provider will implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of PHI" and comply with Security Rule obligations.

#### 1.4 Assessments
Provider agrees to conduct regular compliance assessments under Privacy and Security Rules, with summaries available to Company upon request.

#### 1.5 Mitigation of Risks
Provider must mitigate harmful effects from PHI misuse or disclosure and promptly communicate actions taken to Company.

#### 1.6 Subcontractors
Provider may disclose PHI to subcontractors if they execute binding agreements with substantially similar protections. Provider must conduct appropriate due diligence.

#### 1.7 Books and Records to HHS
"Upon request, Provider will make its books, records, and internal policies and procedures relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary of HHS."

#### 1.8 Audit of Books and Records
Upon reasonable Company request, Provider must make compliance-related records available, except those affecting proprietary rights.

#### 1.9 Individual Requests
Provider will support individual HIPAA rights requests within ten business days maximum, including access, amendment, and disclosure accounting.

#### 1.10 Compliance with Covered Entity's Obligations
When executing Company obligations under Privacy Rule, Provider complies with applicable regulations.

### 2. Company Obligations

#### 2.1 Notice of Privacy Practices
Company provides current privacy notices to Provider upon request.

#### 2.2 Notice of Changes
"Company will notify Provider in a timely manner of any changes to how Company uses or discloses PHI to the extent that the changes impact how Provider uses or discloses PHI."

#### 2.3 Notice of Restrictions
Company must timely notify Provider of individual restrictions on PHI use or disclosure that may impact Provider's obligations.

#### 2.4 Compliance with Laws
Company uses and discloses PHI to Provider only in accordance with HIPAA and applicable law.

### 3. Data Rights & Restrictions

#### 3.1 Offshoring PHI
Subject to applicable limitations, Provider may use and disclose PHI outside the United States.

#### 3.2 De-Identification
Subject to applicable limitations, Provider may de-identify PHI.

#### 3.3 Aggregation
"Provider may aggregate PHI for its own purposes," except as restricted by limitations.

### 4. Breach Notification

#### 4.1 Breach Reporting
"Provider will report to Company within the Breach Notification Period each use or disclosure of PHI not permitted under this BAA" and any Security Incidents involving PHI.

#### 4.2 Unsuccessful Attempts
Periodic unsuccessful access or interference attempts constitute sufficient breach notification.

#### 4.3 Security Incident Reimbursement
Provider reimburses Company for costs reasonably associated with Security Incidents caused by Provider or its subcontractors.

#### 4.4 Confidentiality
Provider will not disclose Security Incident information except as required by law.

### 5. Term & Termination

#### 5.1 Term
"This BAA will start on the BAA Effective Date and will continue in effect until the later of when all obligations of the parties have been met under this BAA or when the Agreement ends or expires."

#### 5.2 Termination
Either party may terminate if other fails to cure material breach within 30 days of notice. Material BAA breaches constitute material Agreement breaches.

#### 5.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, Provider returns or destroys all PHI per Company direction. If return/destruction infeasible, compliance obligations continue and Provider must prevent additional processing.

### 6. Definitions

#### 6.1 Key Terms
- **BAA:** Cover Page incorporating these Standard Terms and referenced policies
- **Breach:** Per HIPAA definition
- **Business Associate:** Per HIPAA definition
- **Covered Entity:** Per HIPAA definition
- **Cover Page:** Signed document identifying parties, incorporating Standard Terms
- **Designated Record Set:** Per HIPAA definition
- **HHS:** U.S. Department of Health and Human Services
- **HIPAA:** Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996
- **Privacy and Security Officials:** Per HIPAA definition
- **Privacy Rule:** Federal privacy regulations (45 CFR Parts 160 and 164, Subparts A & E)
- **Protected Health Information (PHI):** Per HIPAA definition
- **Security Incident:** Per HIPAA definition
- **Security Rule:** Federal security regulations (45 CFR Parts 160 and 164, Subparts A & C)
- **Services:** Products and services provided by Provider under Agreement
- **Subcontractor:** Third party receiving PHI from Provider
- **Variable:** Highlighted, capitalized words with defined meanings
