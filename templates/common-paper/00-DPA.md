# Data Processing Agreement

## COMMON PAPER DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (Version 1.0)

### 1. Processor and Subprocessor Relationships

**Provider as Processor:** When the Customer controls personal data, the Provider acts as a Processor handling that data on the Customer's behalf.

**Provider as Subprocessor:** When the Customer is a Processor of personal data, the Provider functions as a Subprocessor.

### 2. Processing

**Processing Details:** Annex I(B) on the Cover Page outlines the scope, nature, purpose, duration, data categories, and affected data subjects involved in processing activities.

**Processing Instructions:** The Customer directs the Provider to process personal data to: (a) deliver and maintain the Service; (b) fulfill instructions through Service usage; (c) comply with the Agreement; and (d) follow additional written directives acknowledged by the Provider. The Provider must follow these instructions unless Applicable Laws prohibit compliance and must immediately notify the Customer if unable to proceed.

**Processing by Provider:** Processing occurs solely under this DPA terms. Service updates may necessitate modifications to data categories, subjects, safeguards, transfer frequency, processing nature/purpose, and duration, with Customer notification required.

**Customer Processing:** When the Customer functions as a Processor and Provider as Subprocessor, the Customer must comply with all Applicable Laws and controller agreements, including subprocessor requirements.

**Consent to Processing:** The Customer has satisfied and will maintain compliance with all data protection laws regarding providing personal data to the Provider, including disclosures, consents, choices, and safeguards.

**Subprocessors:**
- The Provider cannot transfer personal data to Subprocessors without Customer approval
- The current Approved Subprocessors list identifies each Subprocessor, their location, and processing tasks
- The Provider must provide at least "10 business days in advance and in writing" notice of Subprocessor changes
- Customers have 30 days to object; silence indicates acceptance
- Subprocessor agreements restrict access to data necessary for contracted obligations
- Under GDPR, subprocessor agreements incorporate the same data protection obligations as the main DPA
- The Provider remains fully liable for subprocessor performance and must notify customers of material breaches

### 3. Restricted Transfers

**Authorization:** The Customer authorizes international data transfers outside the EEA, UK, or other relevant territories as needed for Service provision. Where no adequacy decision exists, the Provider implements appropriate safeguards.

**Ex-EEA Transfers:** For GDPR-protected transfers from the EEA to outside territories without adequacy decisions, the parties are deemed to have executed the EEA SCCs by entering this DPA. Module Two applies when Customer is Controller/Provider is Processor; Module Three applies when Customer is Processor/Provider is Subprocessor.

**Ex-UK Transfers:** For UK GDPR-protected transfers from the United Kingdom without adequacy determinations, parties are deemed to have signed the UK Addendum.

**Other International Transfers:** For Swiss law transfers, GDPR references are amended accordingly.

### 4. Security Incident Response

Upon discovering any Security Incident, the Provider will: (a) notify the Customer promptly, not exceeding "72 hours after becoming aware of the Security Incident"; (b) provide timely information as it emerges; and (c) take prompt reasonable containment and investigation steps.

### 5. Audit & Reports

**Audit Rights:** The Provider supplies information demonstrating DPA compliance and permits Customer audits and inspections. Records are maintained for 3 years post-DPA termination.

**Security Reports:** The Provider undergoes regular third-party audits. Upon written request, summaries are provided confidentially.

**Security Due Diligence:** The Provider responds to reasonable Customer information requests confirming compliance, limited to once annually.

### 6. Coordination & Cooperation

**Response to Inquiries:** The Provider notifies the Customer of external inquiries about personal data processing. For valid deletion/opt-out requests, the Provider assists Customer fulfillment.

**DPIAs and DTIAs:** The Provider reasonably assists with mandated data protection impact assessments.

### 7. Deletion of Customer Personal Data

**Deletion by Customer:** The Provider enables Customer deletion consistent with Service functionality.

**Deletion at DPA Expiration:** After DPA expiration, the Provider returns or deletes personal data per Customer instruction.

### 8. Limitation of Liability

**Liability Caps and Damages Waiver:** Total cumulative liability "will be subject to the waivers, exclusions, and limitations of liability stated in the Agreement."

**Related-Party Claims:** Claims may only be brought by the Customer entity party to the Agreement.

### 9. Conflicts Between Documents

Where inconsistencies arise, the following order prevails: (1) EEA SCCs or UK Addendum, (2) this DPA, (3) the Agreement.

### 10. Term of Agreement

The DPA commences when parties agree to a Cover Page and sign/electronically accept the Agreement, continuing until expiration or termination.

### 11. Definitions

- **Applicable Laws:** Laws, rules, regulations, court orders, and binding government requirements
- **Applicable Data Protection Laws:** Laws governing how the Service processes personal information
- **Controller:** Entity determining personal data processing purpose and extent
- **Cover Page:** Signed/electronically accepted document incorporating these DPA Standard Terms
- **Customer Personal Data:** Personal data uploaded/provided to the Provider
- **DPA:** These Standard Terms, the Cover Page, and referenced policies/documents
- **EEA SCCs:** Standard contractual clauses from European Commission Implementing Decision 2021/914
- **European Economic Area (EEA):** EU member states, Norway, Iceland, and Liechtenstein
- **GDPR:** EU Regulation 2016/679
- **Personal Data:** Information per Applicable Data Protection Laws
- **Processing/Process:** Any use or computer operation on Personal Data
- **Processor:** Entity processing personal data on Controller's behalf
- **Report:** Audit reports prepared per Security Policy standards
- **Restricted Transfer:** EEA-to-outside transfers without adequacy determination
- **Security Incident:** A Personal Data Breach per GDPR Article 4
- **Service:** Products/services described in the Agreement
- **Special Category Data:** Data with GDPR Article 9 meaning
- **Subprocessor:** Entity assisting Processors with personal data processing
- **UK GDPR:** EU Regulation 2016/679 as implemented through UK
- **UK Addendum:** International data transfer addendum for Restricted Transfers
