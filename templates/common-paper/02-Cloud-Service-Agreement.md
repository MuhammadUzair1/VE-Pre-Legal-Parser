# Cloud Service Agreement

## COMMON PAPER CLOUD SERVICE AGREEMENT (Version 2.1)

### 1. Service

#### 1.1 Access and Use
During the Subscription Period, the Customer may access and use the Cloud Service and copy Software and Documentation for internal business purposes.

#### 1.2 Support
Provider will provide Technical Support as described in the Order Form.

#### 1.3 User Accounts
The Customer bears responsibility for all account activities and user compliance. Users must safeguard password confidentiality and promptly notify Provider of suspected fraudulent activity.

#### 1.4 Feedback and Usage Data
Customer feedback is voluntary and provided "AS IS." Provider may use feedback freely. Provider may collect and analyze Usage Data for product improvement, but may only share aggregated and anonymized Usage Data.

#### 1.5 Customer Content
Provider may use Customer Content only to provide and maintain the Product. Customer is responsible for accuracy and content.

#### 1.6 Machine Learning
"Usage Data and Customer Content may be used to develop, train, or enhance artificial intelligence or machine learning models." Data must be aggregated and de-identified, though data protection obligations still apply.

### 2. Restrictions & Obligations

#### 2.1 Restrictions on Customer
The Customer cannot:
- Reverse engineer, decompile, or attempt to discover source code
- Sublicense, modify, or misuse the Product
- Develop a competing service
- Conduct security tests without authorization
- Engage in unauthorized access

#### 2.2 Suspension
Provider may suspend access if Customer has:
- Outstanding balance exceeding 30 days
- Material breach of restrictions
- Material misuse of Product

### 3. Privacy & Security

#### 3.1 Personal Data
"Before submitting Personal Data governed by GDPR, Customer must enter into a data processing agreement with Provider."

#### 3.2 Prohibited Data
Customer cannot submit Prohibited Data (health information, financial account numbers, government ID numbers, sensitive categories) unless authorized.

### 4. Payment & Taxes

#### 4.1 Fees
All Fees in U.S. Dollars unless specified otherwise and exclude taxes. "Fees are non-refundable" except for prorated refunds with specific termination rights.

#### 4.2 Invoicing
Provider sends invoices for usage-based Fees in arrears and other Fees in advance.

#### 4.3 Taxes
Customer pays all applicable duties and taxes itemized by Provider, excluding Provider's income taxes.

#### 4.4 Payment Dispute
For good-faith disagreements, Customer must notify Provider before payment is due or within 30 days of automatic payment. Parties work to resolve within 15 days.

### 5. Term & Termination

#### 5.1 Order Form and Agreement
Agreement runs from Order Date through Subscription Period and automatically renews unless non-renewal notice provided.

#### 5.2 Framework Terms
Framework Terms begin on Effective Date and continue for one year or until all Order Forms end, whichever is longer.

#### 5.3 Termination
Either party may terminate immediately if:
- Other party fails to cure material breach within 30 days
- Uncurable material breach occurs
- Business dissolution or insolvency

#### 5.4 Force Majeure
Either party may terminate affected Order Form if Force Majeure Event prevents material operation for 30+ consecutive days.

#### 5.5 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, Customer loses all Product access. Provider deletes Customer Content within 60 days upon request. Both parties return or destroy Confidential Information.

#### 5.6 Survival
Specified sections survive including feedback provisions, payment obligations, and confidentiality.

### 6. Representations & Warranties

#### 6.1 Mutual
Both parties represent they have legal authority, are properly organized, and will comply with Applicable Laws.

#### 6.2 From Customer
"Customer represents and warrants that it, all Users, and anyone submitting Customer Content each have and will continue to have all rights necessary to submit or make available Customer Content."

#### 6.3 From Provider
"Provider represents and warrants to Customer that it will not materially reduce the general functionality of the Cloud Service during the Subscription Period."

#### 6.4 Provider Warranty Remedy
If Provider breaches warranty in Section 6.3, Customer must provide notice within 45 days. Provider has 45 days to restore functionality. If restoration fails, Customer may terminate with prorated refund.

### 7. Disclaimer of Warranties

Provider makes no guarantees regarding safety, security, or error-free operation. "Except for the warranties in Section 6, Provider and Customer each disclaim all other warranties and conditions."

### 8. Limitation of Liability

#### 8.1 Liability Caps
Each party's liability capped at General Cap Amount, or Increased Cap Amount for Increased Claims.

#### 8.2 Damages Waiver
"Under no circumstances will either party be liable for lost profits or revenues, or for consequential, special, indirect, exemplary, punitive, or incidental damages."

#### 8.3 Exceptions
Liability caps do not apply to Increased Claims or Unlimited Claims. Damages waivers do not apply to Increased Claims or Confidentiality breaches.

### 9. Indemnification

#### 9.1 Protection by Provider
Provider indemnifies Customer against Provider Covered Claims including damages and legal expenses.

#### 9.2 Protection by Customer
Customer indemnifies Provider against Customer Covered Claims including damages and legal expenses.

#### 9.3 Procedure
Protected Party must promptly notify Indemnifying Party. Indemnifying Party controls defense and settlement.

#### 9.4 Exclusive Remedy
This indemnification section provides exclusive remedy for Covered Claims.

### 10. Confidentiality

#### 10.1 Non-Use and Non-Disclosure
Recipient will not use or disclose Discloser's Confidential Information. Recipients must use reasonable care protections.

#### 10.2 Exclusions
Confidential Information excludes information:
- Recipient knew before disclosure
- Publicly available
- Received from authorized third parties without confidentiality
- Independently developed

#### 10.3 Required Disclosures
Recipients may disclose when required by law if reasonable advance notice provided.

#### 10.4 Permitted Disclosures
Recipients may disclose to employees, contractors, and representatives with need-to-know, bound by equivalent obligations.

### 11. General Terms

#### 11.1 Entire Agreement
"This Agreement is the only agreement between the parties about its subject and this Agreement supersedes all prior statements."

#### 11.2 Modifications, Severability, and Waiver
Modifications require written signature from both parties. Invalid terms do not invalidate remaining terms.

#### 11.3 Governing Law and Chosen Courts
Governing Law controls interpretation. Parties submit to exclusive jurisdiction of Chosen Courts.

#### 11.4 Injunctive Relief
For Confidentiality breaches or IP violations, non-breaching party may seek equitable relief.

#### 11.5 Non-Exhaustive Remedies
Seeking one remedy does not limit other remedies.

#### 11.6 Assignment
Neither party may assign without written consent, except through mergers or asset sales.

#### 11.7 Beta Products
Beta Products provided "AS IS" without warranties. "Customer acknowledges that Beta Products are experimental and may be modified or removed at Provider's discretion."

#### 11.8 Logo Rights
Provider may identify Customer and use its name and logo in marketing materials.

#### 11.9 Notices
Notices must be written and sent to Notice Address. Deemed given upon confirmed delivery or two days after overnight mailing.

#### 11.10 Independent Contractors
Parties are independent contractors, not agents, partners, or joint venturers.

#### 11.11 No Third-Party Beneficiary
Agreement creates no third-party beneficiary rights.

#### 11.12 Force Majeure
Neither party liable for Force Majeure Event delays, except Customer must still pay Fees.

#### 11.13 Export Controls
Customer cannot export Product in violation of U.S. restrictions.

#### 11.14 Government Rights
Product deemed commercial items under FAR and DFAR regulations.

#### 11.15 Anti-Bribery
Neither party violates anti-bribery laws like FCPA or UK Bribery Act 2010.

#### 11.16 Titles and Interpretation
Section titles for convenience only. "Including" is non-exhaustive.

#### 11.17 Signature
Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including electronically.

### 12. Definitions

- **Affiliate:** Entity controlling, controlled by, or under common control (50%+ voting stock)
- **Agreement:** Order Form governed by Framework Terms
- **Applicable Data Protection Laws:** Laws governing personal information processing
- **Applicable Laws:** Laws, regulations, court orders of relevant authorities
- **Beta Product:** Early or prerelease feature identified as beta
- **Cloud Service:** Product described in Order Form
- **Confidential Information:** Information identified as confidential or reasonably understood as such
- **Cover Page:** Signed document incorporating Standard Terms
- **Covered Claim:** Provider Covered Claim or Customer Covered Claim
- **Customer Content:** Data submitted by Customer or Users
- **Discloser:** Party providing Confidential Information
- **Documentation:** Usage manuals and instructional materials
- **Embargoed Country:** Country where laws restrict export/import
- **Feedback:** Suggestions about Product
- **Fees:** Amounts described in Order Form
- **Force Majeure Event:** Unforeseen event outside reasonable control
- **Framework Terms:** Standard Terms, Key Terms, and referenced policies
- **GDPR:** EU Regulation 2016/679
- **High Risk Activity:** Situations where failure could cause death or injury
- **Indemnifying Party:** Party providing Covered Claim protection
- **Key Terms:** Cover Page with key legal details and Variables
- **OFAC:** U.S. Department of Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control
- **Order Form:** Cover Page with key business details
- **Personal Data:** Per Applicable Data Protection Laws
- **Product:** Cloud Service, Software, and Documentation
- **Prohibited Data:** Health info, financial account numbers, government ID numbers, sensitive categories
- **Protected Party:** Party receiving Covered Claim protection
- **Recipient:** Party receiving Confidential Information
- **Software:** Client-side software or applications
- **Standard Terms:** Common Paper Cloud Service Agreement Standard Terms Version 2.1
- **Usage Data:** Data about Product provision, use, and performance
- **User:** Individual using Product on Customer's behalf
- **Variable:** Highlighted, capitalized word with defined meaning
