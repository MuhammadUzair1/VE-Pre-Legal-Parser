# Common Paper Legal Agreement Templates

This directory contains standardized legal agreement templates from Common Paper, a collection of freely usable legal templates created collaboratively by over 40 attorneys from technology vendors, procurement teams, boutique firms, and major law firms.

## Overview

- **License**: CC BY 4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0) - Free to use and modify
- **Organization**: https://github.com/commonpaper
- **Versioning**: Version 1 templates remain permanently unchanged; future versions created only for legal or use-case updates
- **Implementation Model**: Two-part execution using Cover Page + Standard Terms by reference

## How These Templates Work

1. **Cover Page + Standard Terms Model**
   - Provider customizes a Cover Page with business-specific terms
   - Cover Page incorporates Standard Terms by reference
   - Standard Terms remain unchanged and published online

2. **Customization Points**
   - Each agreement has bracketed sections for customization
   - Cover Pages include fields for:
     - Parties and contact information
     - Business terms (fees, duration, services)
     - Legal terms (governing law, jurisdiction)
     - Custom modifications

3. **Execution**
   - Sign the Cover Page
   - Sign/accept the agreement
   - Standard Terms are incorporated by reference

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Review**: Read the entire agreement before use
2. **Customize**: Use the Cover Page to add business-specific terms
3. **Legal Review**: Have legal counsel review customizations
4. **Execute**: Sign the Cover Page which incorporates Standard Terms
5. **Reference**: Point to the online published version of Standard Terms
